Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · Plateonic is made by Jonah Herold · support@plateonic.app

The short version

Plateonic keeps your training on your iPhone. We run no servers for it, so we don't receive your workouts, your body details or anything else you enter. There are no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your training: workouts, sets, weights, reps, notes, pain flags, your exercises and equipment.
  • About you, if you enter it: bodyweight, birth year and sex, used to set first weights and score your strength.
  • Settings and preferences.

All of it is stored on your iPhone. The app also writes a daily backup file to its own folder on your iPhone (Files › Plateonic › Backups). If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, Apple includes the app's data in those backups under Apple's terms.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, Plateonic reads your bodyweight, date of birth and sex from Health, and saves each finished workout to Health. Health data stays on your iPhone, is used only for the features above, and is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can turn this off in Plateonic's Settings or in the Health app at any time.

Importing and exporting

Files you import (for example a Hevy, Strong or Fitbod export) are read on your iPhone. Exports and backups go only where you send them.

Crash reports and support

iOS gives the app crash and hang reports. They stay on your iPhone. If you tap Settings › Send my data to support, the app prepares a backup of your data, the app version and phone model, and those reports, and you choose where to send them. Nothing is sent unless you do that.

Separately, if you agreed to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple gives us anonymous crash and usage statistics. TestFlight builds send Apple crash logs and any feedback you submit.

What we don't do

No account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking libraries, no sale or sharing of personal data.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase all data removes everything Plateonic holds on your iPhone, including its backups. Deleting the app does the same. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

Children

Plateonic is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We'll update this page when the app changes how it handles data, and change the date above.
