Support

Write to support@plateonic.app. We usually reply within two working days.

Before you write

  • Something looks wrong with your data or a suggestion. In Plateonic open Profile › Settings › Help › Send my data to support and send the files it prepares. They hold a backup of your training, the app version and phone model, and any crash reports.
  • Moving to a new phone. Settings › Export everything on the old phone, then Restore from backup on the new one. Daily backups are also in Files › Plateonic › Backups.
  • Coming from Hevy, Strong or Fitbod. Export a CSV from that app, then Settings › Import. Importing the same file twice adds nothing.
  • Rest alerts don't arrive. Check iOS Settings › Notifications › Plateonic. Rest alerts are time-sensitive, so they can come through a Focus if you allow that.
  • Starting over. Settings › Erase all data removes everything on this iPhone and opens setup.

Health and safety

Plateonic isn't medical advice. Stop if something hurts, and see a doctor or physio about pain that lasts.
